[หัวเรื่อง - จัดกึ่งกลาง]
Lecture Note 8: Divisibility and Primes
Foundations of Mathematics

[หัวข้อระดับ 1]
Divisibility

[นิยาม - คำว่า divides เน้นด้วย \emph]
Let {a} and {b} be integers with {a not-equal 0}. We say that {a}
divides {b}, and write {a | b}, if there is an integer {k} such
that {b = ak}.

[ตัวอย่าง]
We have {3 | 12} because {12 = 3 · 4}, but {5 does-not-divide 12} because no
integer {k} satisfies {12 = 5k}.

[บทตั้ง (Lemma)]
If {a | b} and {a | c}, then {a | (b + c)}.

[บทพิสูจน์]
Write {b = ak} and {c = al} with integers {k} and {l}. Then
{b + c = a(k + l)}, and {k + l} is an integer.

[ทฤษฎีบท ชื่อ Transitivity]
If {a | b} and {b | c}, then {a | c}.

[บทพิสูจน์]
By definition there are integers {k} and {l} with {b = ak} and {c = bl}.
Substituting the first equation into the second gives
[แสดงผลแยกบรรทัด] c = (ak)l = a(kl).
Since {kl} is an integer, {a | c}.

[บทแทรก (Corollary)]
If {a | b}, then {a | bc} for every integer {c}.

[บทพิสูจน์]
Since {b | bc}, this follows from Theorem [อ้างถึงทฤษฎีบท Transitivity].

[หัวข้อระดับ 1]
Prime Numbers

[นิยาม - คำว่า prime เน้นด้วย \emph]
An integer {p > 1} is prime if its only positive divisors are {1}
and {p}.

[ตัวอย่าง]
The first primes are {2, 3, 5, 7, 11} and {13}. The number {1} is not
prime, and {2} is the only even prime.

[ทฤษฎีบท ชื่อ Euclid]
There are infinitely many prime numbers.

[บทพิสูจน์]
Suppose that there are only finitely many primes {p_1, p_2, ..., p_n},
and consider
[แสดงผลแยกบรรทัด] N = p_1 p_2 ... p_n + 1 .
Some prime {p_i} divides {N}, and {p_i} also divides
{p_1 p_2 ... p_n}. By Lemma [อ้างถึงบทตั้งในหัวข้อ 1] applied to {N} and
{-p_1 p_2 ... p_n}, the prime {p_i} divides their sum, which is
[แสดงผลแยกบรรทัด] N - p_1 p_2 ... p_n = 1 .
This is impossible, because no prime divides {1}.

[ข้อสังเกต (ไม่มีหมายเลข)]
The proof of Theorem [อ้างถึงทฤษฎีบท Euclid] does not say that {N} itself is
prime. For example, {2 · 3 · 5 · 7 · 11 · 13 + 1 =
30031 = 59 · 509}.
