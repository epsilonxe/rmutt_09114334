\chapter{Before You Start}
Every knot in this book can be tied with a one-metre piece of rope.
Practise each knot ten times before you move on to the next one.

\chapter{Basic Knots}

\section{The Square Knot}
Right over left, then left over right. Use it to tie two ends of the
same rope together, for example around a bundle of sticks.

\section{The Bowline}
The bowline makes a loop that does not slip. Sailors call it the king
of knots.

\chapter{Hitches}

\section{The Clove Hitch}
A clove hitch ties a rope to a post. It is quick to tie and quick to
undo.

\chapter{Answers to the Quiz}
1. Square knot. 2. Bowline. 3. Clove hitch.
