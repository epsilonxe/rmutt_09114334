\section*{Maths as plain text}
The area of a circle is pi r2, where r is the radius.

The solutions of ax2 + bx + c = 0 are x = (-b + sqrt(b2 - 4ac)) / 2a.

The sum 1 + 2 + ... + n equals n(n+1)/2.

The angle alpha is between 0 and 90 degrees.
