Calculus Formula Sheet
Mathematics for Science, Week 6

[หัวข้อไม่มีหมายเลข]
Numbers, Sets and Powers

We write {N} for the natural numbers, {Z} for the integers and {R} for the real numbers, so that {N subset Z subset R}. An interval such as {[a, b] = { x in R | a <= x <= b }} contains its end points, while {(a, b)} does not.

[ตัว N, Z, R เป็นอักษรตัวทึบแบบ blackboard bold]

For {a > 0} and any real numbers {m} and {n},

[แสดงผลแยกบรรทัด - 4 สูตรเว้นระยะด้วย \qquad]
a^m a^n = a^(m+n),   (a^m)^n = a^(mn),   nth-root(a) = a^(1/n),   a^(-n) = 1/(a^n).

[หัวข้อไม่มีหมายเลข]
Limits and Derivatives

With the Greek letter {theta} for an angle, two limits appear again and again:

[แสดงผลแยกบรรทัด]
lim (theta -> 0) sin(theta)/theta = 1   and   lim (n -> infinity) (1 + 1/n)^n = e.

If {f(x) = x^n} then {f'(x) = n x^(n-1)}. The quotient rule says

[แสดงผลแยกบรรทัด]
(u/v)' = (u'v - uv')/(v^2),   v not-equal 0.

[หัวข้อไม่มีหมายเลข]
Integrals

For {n not-equal -1},

[แสดงผลแยกบรรทัด]
integral x^n dx = x^(n+1)/(n+1) + C,   integral from 0 to pi of sin(theta) d(theta) = 2,   integral from -infinity to infinity of e^(-x^2) dx = sqrt(pi).

[หัวข้อไม่มีหมายเลข]
Sums and Series

The sum of the first {n} natural numbers and the geometric series with ratio {|r| < 1} are

[แสดงผลแยกบรรทัด]
sum (k=1 to n) k = n(n+1)/2   and   sum (k=0 to infinity) r^k = 1 + r + r^2 + ... = 1/(1-r).

In a sentence a sum is kept small, {sum (k=1 to n) k}, and {sum (k=1 to n) k in display size} forces display size. The binomial theorem expands a power of a sum:

[แสดงผลแยกบรรทัด]
(a + b)^n = sum (k=0 to n) binomial(n, k) a^(n-k) b^k.
