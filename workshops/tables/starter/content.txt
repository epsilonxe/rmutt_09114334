[หัวข้อระดับ 1]
Allergy Notes

Please tell the staff about any allergy before you order. Table 3 lists the most common questions.

[ตาราง 3 - คำบรรยาย]
Common allergy questions.

[หัวตาราง - ตัวหนา]
Ingredient | What you should know
[ข้อมูล - คอลัมน์ขวาเป็นย่อหน้า]
Milk | Every milk drink can be made with oat milk or soy milk at no extra cost.
Nuts | The almond cookies and the banana cake contain nuts. All cakes are baked in the same kitchen.
Gluten | Only the coconut jelly and the fruit cup are gluten free.

[หัวข้อระดับ 1]
Orders for Groups

Clubs and classes can order drinks and snacks for meetings of up to forty people. Please order at the counter at least two days in advance and pay half of the total when you order. We deliver to any building on campus free of charge between 08:00 and 16:00.
