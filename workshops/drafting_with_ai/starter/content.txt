[หัวข้อระดับ 1]
Right Triangles with Whole Sides

A Pythagorean triple (ตัวเอียง) is a list of three positive integers (a, b, c) with a^2 + b^2 = c^2. By the theorem of Pythagoras, such numbers are the side lengths of a right triangle, as in Figure <<ref fig:triangle>>. Table <<ref tab:triples>> lists the five triples with the smallest hypotenuse c.

[รูปภาพ P2: ไฟล์ triangle.pdf]
Caption: A right triangle with legs a, b and hypotenuse c
Label: fig:triangle

[ตาราง P3]
Caption: The five Pythagorean triples with the smallest c

[หัวข้อระดับ 1]
Euclid's Formula

Most triples in Table <<ref tab:triples>> come from one formula, which was already known to Euclid.

[ทฤษฎีบท P4: หมายเลข 1 ชื่อ Euclid, Label: thm:euclid]
Let m and n be integers with m > n > 0. Then
    a = m^2 - n^2,   b = 2mn,   c = m^2 + n^2
form a Pythagorean triple.

[บทพิสูจน์ P5]
Since m > n > 0, all three numbers are positive. Expanding gives
    a^2 + b^2 = (m^2 - n^2)^2 + (2mn)^2
              = m^4 - 2m^2 n^2 + n^4 + 4m^2 n^2
              = m^4 + 2m^2 n^2 + n^4
              = (m^2 + n^2)^2 = c^2.

[ย่อหน้าธรรมดา]
For example, m = 2, n = 1 gives (3, 4, 5) and m = 3, n = 2 gives (5, 12, 13). The triple (9, 12, 15) does not come from Theorem <<ref thm:euclid>> directly: it is three times (3, 4, 5).
