[หัวเรื่อง - จัดกึ่งกลาง]
Pour-Over Coffee
A beginner's brewing guide

[ชิดขวา]
Campus Coffee Club
Issue No. 1

[ย่อหน้าที่ 1 - ไม่มีการเยื้อง]
Pour-over is one of the simplest ways to brew clean and bright coffee at home. You pour hot water slowly over ground coffee in a paper filter, and gravity does the rest. The method is cheap, quiet, and gives you full control over every variable.

[ย่อหน้าที่ 2]
This guide follows the brewing recommendations of the Specialty Coffee Association. Read it once before you start; the whole process takes about four minutes.

[หัวข้อ]
What you need

[รายการ]
A dripper and a paper filter
15 g of medium-fine ground coffee
250 g of water, just off the boil
A kettle, a scale, and a timer

[หัวข้อ]
Brewing steps

[รายการแบบมีลำดับ]
Rinse the paper filter with hot water and throw that water away.
Add the coffee and shake the dripper gently to level the bed.
Pour about 40 g of water and wait 30 seconds.
    [รายการย่อยของข้อ 3]
    This step is called the bloom.
    Fresh coffee will bubble as it releases gas.
Pour the rest of the water slowly in small circles.
Remove the dripper when the water has drained, and enjoy.

[หัวข้อ]
Troubleshooting

[รายการ - ป้ายกำกับ ? ? !]
Sour: grind finer, or use hotter water.
Bitter: grind coarser, or pour faster.
Never reuse a filter or old grounds.

[จัดกึ่งกลาง]
Tip: change only one thing at a time.
