Notes on Rivers

Rivers carry rain and melted snow from high ground down to the sea.
Along the way they shape valleys, feed farms and connect cities.

Where Rivers Begin

Most rivers start as many small streams that join together.

Mountain Springs

Water that soaks into a mountain can come out again as a spring.

Melting Glaciers

In summer, glaciers release a steady flow of cold water.

Where Rivers End

A river ends where it meets a lake, another river or the sea.

Further Reading

Ask the library for books about the river in your own province.
